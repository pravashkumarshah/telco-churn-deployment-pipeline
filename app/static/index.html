STAGE 01

Customer Intake & Schema

Customer telemetry enters via typed Pydantic contracts. Form fields handle missing strings safely, and unknown CRM categories are ignored without failing.

STAGE 02

ML Preprocessing & Model

Financials are scaled via standard normalizers, empty values are median-imputed, and a cross-validated Random Forest outputs churn probability.

STAGE 03

The 0.32 Business Cutoff

Losing a churner costs $250 vs $40 for outreach. Cost-minimization sets the decision trigger at 0.32, catching 89% of all churning accounts.

STAGE 04

Retention Simulator

STAGE 05

PSI Drift Guardrails

The Population Stability Index screens incoming customer distributions against the training baseline. PSI > 0.20 triggers an investigation warning.

Engineering & Mathematical Documentation

Why is the Decision Cutoff 0.32 instead of 0.50?

Standard classifiers assume symmetric penalties (a False Positive costs the same as a False Negative). In subscription telecom:

Cost(FN) = $250.00 → Missed churner cancels; total recurring lifetime revenue lost.
Cost(FP) = $40.00 → Loyal customer receives a proactive loyalty retention discount.
Total Cost = (FN × 250.0) + (FP × 40.0)

During 3-fold cross-validation on the validation split, the threshold was evaluated across the entire 0.05 to 0.95 probability spectrum. At t = 0.32, the business loss achieves its global minimum, capturing 89.04% of all churning accounts.

Population Stability Index (PSI) Formula

PSI calculates statistical divergence between empirical training baseline distributions ($B$) and recent scoring batches ($A$):

PSI = ∑ [ (Actual% - Baseline%) × ln(Actual% / Baseline%) ]
  • PSI < 0.10: Minimal or no material drift; distribution is stable.
  • 0.10 ≤ PSI ≤ 0.20: Moderate shift; monitor data pipelines and seasonality.
  • PSI > 0.20: Significant drift; alerts triggers human review (do not auto-retrain).
